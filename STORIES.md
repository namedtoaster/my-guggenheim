# Stories

Museum stories and exhibition stories from people who use My Guggenheim. Each one
is shared with permission and signed with only the name its writer chose.

[Add yours](https://github.com/namedtoaster/my-guggenheim/issues/new?template=my-story.yml).

---

## The wall text that started it

*The author, October 2026*

I used to try to win at museums. Then, in front of a wall text at the Guggenheim, I
noticed I wasn't trying anymore. I was reading what I could and getting what I got.
I took a picture of the wall and wrote a note: make the thing that writes this for
everyone's photos. This repo is that note.
