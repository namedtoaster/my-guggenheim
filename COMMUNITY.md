# On view elsewhere

Exhibitions people have made with My Guggenheim and posted themselves. This page holds
**links only**: no one's photos are ever copied into this repository. Each show stays
on the poster's own account, where they can edit it or take it down whenever they like,
and a link here is removed on request.

[Add yours](https://github.com/namedtoaster/my-guggenheim/issues/new?template=share-exhibition.yml),
or post it with **#MyGuggenheim** and we may ask if we can link it.
